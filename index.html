Types of Angles

Type: Acute Angle

This is an angle between 0° and 90°

Acute

0° - 90°

Example: 30°

Right

Exactly 90°

Example: 90°

Obtuse

90° - 180°

Example: 120°

Straight

Exactly 180°

Example: 180°

Reflex

180° - 360°

Example: 270°

Full

Exactly 360°

Example: 360°

Perpendicular Lines

Instructions:

  1. Click on the canvas to place a point on the line
  2. Click "Construct Perpendicular" to draw a line perpendicular to the base line
  3. The right angle symbol (⊥) shows where the lines meet at 90°

Perpendicular Lines: Two lines that intersect at exactly 90°

Symbol: ⊥

Parallel Lines

Instructions:

  1. Click "Add Parallel Line" to add lines parallel to the base line
  2. Parallel lines never intersect and are always the same distance apart
  3. They are marked with the symbol ∥

Parallel Lines: Two lines that never intersect and maintain equal distance

Symbol: ∥
